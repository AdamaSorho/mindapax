Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during our business days. For urgent matters, please call our customer support line.

Can I schedule a demo of your platform?

Yes! We offer free demos of the Mindapax platform. Please fill out the contact form and select "Partnership Opportunity" in the inquiry type, and our team will arrange a convenient time for your demo.

How can I join your team as a wellness professional?

We're always looking for qualified professionals to join our platform. Please submit your inquiry with "Career Information" selected, and we'll provide you with information about our onboarding process.
