About Mindapax

Our Story

Mindapax was founded in 2022 with a revolutionary idea: that mental, physical, and financial wellness are deeply interconnected, and that true well-being requires addressing all three aspects.

Our founder, Dr. Elena Rodriguez, saw firsthand how financial stress impacted her therapy clients' mental health, and how mental health challenges often manifested in physical symptoms. She envisioned a platform where people could access comprehensive care in one place.

Today, Mindapax connects thousands of individuals with qualified professionals across these three vital dimensions of wellness, helping people live healthier, more balanced lives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how Mindapax works

How does Mindapax verify its professionals?

All professionals on our platform undergo a rigorous verification process including credential checks, background screening, and professional reference verification. We only accept licensed or certified practitioners in their respective fields.

Is my information secure on Mindapax?

Absolutely. We maintain the highest standards of data security and privacy. Our platform is HIPAA-compliant, and we use end-to-end encryption for all communications. Your privacy is our top priority.

Can I switch between different types of professionals?

Yes! One of the benefits of Mindapax is the ability to access different types of professionals all on one platform. You can work with a therapist, financial coach, and wellness expert simultaneously or switch as your needs change.

How do I get started with Mindapax?

Getting started is easy! Simply create a free account, complete a brief wellness assessment, and you'll be matched with professionals who meet your specific needs. You can then review profiles, book sessions, and begin your wellness journey.
